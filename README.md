# metrix

> Turning statistical complexity into clarity, one calculation at a time.

## The Story Behind metrix

Picture this: It's 2 AM, and you're staring at a spreadsheet filled with numbers for your research project. You know you need to run a Chi-Square test, but the formulas are intimidating, the manual calculations are error-prone, and the expensive statistical software feels like overkill for what you need. Sound familiar?

That's exactly why metrix exists.

I built metrix because I've been there—watching students and researchers struggle with mathematical statistics not because they don't understand the concepts, but because the tools are either too complicated, too expensive, or simply not accessible when you need them most.

## What metrix Does

metrix is your friendly statistical companion. It takes the heavy lifting out of mathematical statistics, letting you focus on what really matters: understanding your data and drawing meaningful conclusions from your research.

Think of it as having a statistics expert in your pocket—always available, never judgmental, and ready to help you validate your hypotheses with confidence.

## What You Can Do Right Now

**Chi-Square Goodness of Fit Test**  
Wondering if your observed data matches what you expected? This test helps you determine whether the differences between your observed and expected values are due to chance or if there's something more significant happening in your data.

Perfect for:
- Testing if dice are fair
- Analyzing survey response distributions
- Validating genetic inheritance patterns
- Checking if your sample represents the population

## What's Coming Next

This is just the beginning. metrix is growing into a comprehensive statistical toolkit that will include:
- More hypothesis tests (t-tests, ANOVA, correlation tests)
- Descriptive statistics calculators
- Probability distributions
- Regression analysis tools
- Data visualization features

Each tool will be designed with the same philosophy: make statistics accessible, understandable, and actually useful for real-world research and learning.
